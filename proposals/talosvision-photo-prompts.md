# TalosVision — Photo Prompts for Product Cards

> Minimal, transaction-focused compositions. Each prompt isolates the core action/moment of the product. Surroundings are abstract, blurred, or cropped out — the frame is the interaction itself.

---

## 01 — TalosVision Optic
**Attendance Management + Vision-Powered 2FA**

```
Close-up of a face at the point of identity verification. A translucent cyan facial-recognition mesh grid overlays the face, with floating UI elements: a dynamic QR code shimmering beside the cheek, an NFC tap icon pulsing near the jawline, and a "VERIFIED" status badge in the corner. Dark gradient background, no visible environment. Photorealistic, minimal, cyber-blue accent tones.
```

---

**Style notes for all prompts:**
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 or 4:5 (product-card friendly)
- Color space: deep navy/abyss background (#040D18), cyan (#00D4FF) and azure (#3B82F6) accents
- Lighting: low-key, rim-lit interfaces, no ambient environmental lighting
- Render style: photorealistic UI/HUD overlays on real subjects; dark tech aesthetic
- Composition: tight crop, subject-forward, environment abstracted or absent

## Target Verticals

> Minimal, abstract compositions for each vertical card in the Target Verticals section. No identifiable people, no full environments — just the distilled security/vision data layer on a dark abyss canvas. Each image captures the essence of the sector through an abstracted machine-vision lens.

---

### 01 — Maritime & Shipping
**Crew presence, access control, perimeter protection, fleet-wide aggregation**

```
A container vessel reduced to a cyan wireframe schematic on a dark abyss plane. Crew-presence dots pulse at key stations across the deck. Access-point gate icons glow at hatchways and gangways. A translucent perimeter ring encircles the vessel with a "SECURED" status badge. In the upper corner, a fleet-aggregation tile shows multiple ship silhouettes with green health indicators. No water, no sky — purely the data abstraction of a secured maritime asset. Dark navy background, cyan and azure linework, minimal photorealistic HUD aesthetic.
```

---

### 02 — Healthcare & Hospitals
**Staff attendance, restricted area monitoring, visitor management**

```
A hospital corridor reduced to a cyan architectural line-drawing — clean, minimal geometric outlines of doorways and zones. A badge-scan ring pulses at a restricted-access door with a "CLEARED" confirmation. A visitor-count ticker floats mid-frame with a subtle upward trend. Staff-shift status rings orbit in the background — cyan for active, dimmed for off-shift. A pharmacy-cabinet icon glows behind an extra access-control boundary. No walls, no floors, no people — only the security topology. Dark abyss backdrop, cyan wireframe style.
```

A guard-post status array sits in the lower quadrant — all dots cyan (active). One outer-ring breach indicator blinks amber then resolves. No terrain, no buildings — pure abstracted defence grid on a black abyss field.

---

**Style notes for all vertical prompts:**
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 or 4:5 (product-card friendly)
- Color space: deep navy/abyss background (#040D18), cyan (#00D4FF) and azure (#3B82F6) accents with amber (#F59E0B) for alerts/warnings
- Lighting: low-key, no ambient environmental lighting; all light emitted from the data/HUD elements themselves
- Render style: photorealistic data-visualisation and HUD overlays; dark cyber-tech aesthetic
- Composition: tight crop, no identifiable people, no full architectural environments — subject is always the abstracted security/vision data layer
- For each vertical: the image should feel like a single-frame capture of the TalosVision intelligence layer specific to that sector — as if the platform's monitoring interface were rendered directly onto the sector's conceptual space
