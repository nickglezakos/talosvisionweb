The Guardian Automaton of Crete — Reforged for the Age of Vision

The Myth of Talos — Talos (Τάλως) was a giant automaton forged of bronze by Hephaestus. Gifted to King Minos of Crete, he circled the island three times daily, hurling boulders at approaching ships. A single bronze nail sealed his only vein — his sole vulnerability.

TalosVision inherits this legacy: an unblinking sentinel powered not by ichor but by intelligence. Where the bronze giant circled an island, our platform surveils perimeters. Where his eyes scanned the horizon, our machine vision detects, counts, and protects — always on, always watching, never resting.
