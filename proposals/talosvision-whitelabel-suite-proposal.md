## 1. Executive Summary

Talos Group has built its reputation on delivering **certified, life-critical e-Health ecosystems** to governments, maritime fleets, hospitals, and armed forces across Europe, the Middle East, and the United States. Our portfolio — powered by FDA-approved, CE-certified, and ISO-compliant technologies — transforms raw clinical data into actionable intelligence.

**TalosVision** extends this mission into the domain of **machine vision and real-time operational intelligence**. It is a comprehensive, on-premises whitelabel suite that converts moderate, off-the-shelf hardware into powerful, AI-driven vision nodes — capable of face detection, people counting, demographic profiling, access control, perimeter protection, and multi-site command aggregation.

Just as Talos Group brought telemedicine to the most demanding environments on earth, **TalosVision brings always-on, intelligent sight** to the facilities, fleets, and enterprises that need it most.

> **"Don't just protect your assets — see them protected."**

## 3. Application Suite

TalosVision comprises **six specialised applications**, each engineered to address a distinct operational challenge with precision and intelligence.

### 3.1 TalosVision Optic

**Function:** Attendance Management + Vision-Powered Two-Factor Authentication

TalosVision Optic provides next-level staff presence management by combining **dynamic QR/NFC technology** with **facial recognition** for ironclad dual-factor verification. Designed for environments where knowing exactly who is present — and when — is non-negotiable.

#### Key Features

- **Dynamic QR / NFC Check-In/Out** — Intelligent first-level authentication via rotating tokens
- **Vision 2FA** — Combine QR/NFC presentation with real-time facial recognition for dual-factor verification
- **Fast-Gate Scenarios** — Asynchronous two-factor vision applied on fast-moving gate environments
- **Scheduled Attendance** — Assign schedules and members to rooms, zones, or facilities
- **Automated Reporting** — Comprehensive attendance reports with full event history
- **Unauthorised Presence Alerts** — Instant notification if unrecognised individuals enter restricted areas
- **Guardian Notifications** — Automatic attendance reports for dependents (e.g., minors in schools) sent to family members or sponsors in real time

#### Target Users

- Schools, universities, and educational institutions
- Gyms, fitness centres, and wellness facilities
- Industrial plants and manufacturing sites
- Corporate offices and co-working spaces
- Hospitals and healthcare facilities
- Maritime vessels and offshore installations

#### Operating Modes

Zone Mode · Portal Mode

### 3.2 TalosVision Pulse

**Function:** Vision-Adaptive Digital Signage

TalosVision Pulse transforms traditional digital displays into **intelligent, data-driven marketing platforms**. By analysing the real-time demographic profile of viewers — including age, gender, and group size — Pulse automatically serves targeted content and switches playlists in milliseconds, maximising engagement and proving return on investment.

#### Key Features

- **Read the Room** — Cameras assess audience demographics in real time and adapt content instantly
- **Auto-Trigger Playlists** — Trigger promotions, emergency messages, or informational content automatically based on who is watching
- **Demographic-Driven Delivery** — Serve age- and gender-targeted advertisements without manual intervention
- **Prove ROI** — Measure every impression: views, dwell time, engagement rate, and conversion funnels
- **Content Management Portal** — User-friendly web interface for uploading and assigning targeted media
- **Optional People Activity Insights** — Add-on for comprehensive footfall-to-engagement analytics

#### Target Users

- Retail shops and shopping malls
- Airports and transport hubs
- Advertising agencies and media networks
- Event venues and stadiums
- Hospitality and hotel lobbies
- Corporate reception areas

#### Operating Modes

Focus Mode · Portal Mode · Zone Mode

### 3.3 TalosVision Sonar

**Function:** Access Control + Area Monitoring

TalosVision Sonar delivers comprehensive security and operational intelligence for gates, entrances, exits, corridors, and defined zones. It provides real-time data on occupancy, crowd density, and movement patterns while triggering instant security alerts for anomalous behaviour — all from a single dashboard.

#### Key Features

- **Any Badge, Any Pass** — Support printed tickets, dynamic mobile passes, or NFC badges — your choice
- **Real-Time People Counters** — Live occupancy data with zone-level granularity
- **Demographic Insights** — Age and gender breakdowns for all monitored areas
- **Activity Heatmaps** — Visualise movement patterns, dwell zones, and traffic flows
- **Advanced Behaviour Metrics** — Crowd build-up detection, loitering alerts, tailgating flags
- **Facial Recognition for Persons of Interest** — Instant matching against whitelists and watchlists
- **Live Video Streaming with Annotations** — People activity overlays on all camera feeds
- **Flexible Video Storage** — Configurable retention policies on local or NAS storage
- **Compliance-Ready Reports** — Timestamped video records, occupancy logs, and incident documentation

#### Target Users

- Commercial office buildings
- Airports, ports, and public transport hubs
- Industrial facilities and logistics centres
- Event organisers and stadium operators
- Government and municipal buildings
- Museums, galleries, and public attractions

#### Operating Modes

Portal Mode

### 3.4 TalosVision Aether

**Function:** Wearable Mobile Security (Goggles + Drone)

TalosVision Aether is an advanced, mobile-first security solution that empowers field agents, security personnel, and first responders through **wearable camera eyewear** and **drone-based aerial surveillance**. It provides on-the-go alerts, real-time person-of-interest detection, and seamless data streaming to a central command unit — putting the power of a full security operations centre into a wearable form factor.

#### Key Features

- **Instant Audio Alerts** — Agent receives immediate notification if a person of interest enters their field of view
- **Central Command Integration** — All activity annotations streamed to a main computing unit for team-wide coordination
- **Collaborative Marking** — Team operator can add a detected person to the "persons of interest" group directly from an agent's live feed
- **On-Premises Secure** — Whitelists, watchlists, and demographic profiling all processed locally — fully private
- **Multi-Camera Mesh** — Parallel detection across entire venues, coordinated through a single dashboard
- **Sub-100ms Face Match** — Near-instantaneous recognition even in crowded environments
- **Drone Mode** — Extend surveillance to aerial perspectives for large-area coverage and mobile perspective tracking

#### Target Users

- Security agencies and private security firms
- Law enforcement and border control
- Event security teams and VIP protection details
- Military and defence installations
- Critical infrastructure protection

#### Operating Modes

Wearable Mode · Aerial Mode

TalosVision Nexus ensures that security personnel remain **continuously vigilant** at their watch stations. By automatically monitoring guard activity and inactivity patterns, Nexus provides business owners and security managers with peace of mind — guaranteeing consistent oversight during every shift, with immediate escalation when attention falters.

- **Customisable Alert Criteria** — Define thresholds for acceptable inactivity periods and trigger conditions
- **Instant Notifications** — Push alerts to supervisors and stakeholders the moment criteria are breached
- **Live Video Streams with Annotations** — Full people activity overlays on all monitored feeds
- **Shift Handover Reports** — Automated timelines and evidence exports — audit-ready 24/7
- **Multi-Post Monitoring** — Monitor multiple watch stations simultaneously from a single interface

#### Target Users

- Security firms and guarding companies
- Corporate offices with dedicated security staff
- Industrial plants and manufacturing facilities
- Government buildings and embassies
- Data centres and critical infrastructure sites

#### Operating Modes

Zone Mode

### 3.6 TalosVision Spectral

**Function:** Multi-Site Aggregation & Centralised Command

TalosVision Spectral provides **centralised management and aggregated reporting** across all TalosVision installations in a multi-site or multi-fleet network. Designed to operate over VPN, Spectral communicates with member installations via their internal VPN node URLs — all cross-installation queries travel transparently within a private network, requiring no public internet exposure.

#### Key Features

- **6 Aggregated Report Types** — Summary, Presence, Gate Activity, Camera Events, Demographics, and Logs — querying all member installations in parallel
- **Matrix Groups & Installation Membership** — Full CRUD for groups and member installations with multi-install licensing enforcement
- **User Directory & SSO (JWT-Based)** — Centralised user management with role-based access control across all installations
- **Degraded Mode** — If some installations are unreachable, reports still return partial data — no single point of failure
- **Result Caching** — 60-second TTL report cache for responsive dashboards across distributed sites
- **Time-Range Filtering** — All reports support ISO-8601 timestamp query parameters for precise historical analysis
- **Cross-Site Analytics** — Compare performance, incidents, and trends across locations
- **Unified Alert Management** — Centralised incident queue with cross-installation correlation

#### Target Users

- Multi-site enterprises and franchise networks
- Security operations centres (SOCs)
- Maritime fleet operators
- Airport and port authorities
- Government agencies with distributed facilities
- Any organisation managing multiple TalosVision installations

#### Operating Modes

All Modes — aggregated across member installations

## 8. Strategic Rationale

### Why Machine Vision, Why Now

Talos Group's core competency lies in delivering **certified, life-critical technology ecosystems** to the world's most demanding environments. TalosVision is the natural evolution of this mission:

1. **Adjacent Market Expansion** — Machine vision sits directly alongside telemedicine and e-Health in the broader operational technology landscape. 2. **Shared Value Proposition** — Both Talos e-Health and TalosVision share core DNA: on-premises deployment, zero cloud dependency, VPN-secured remote access, and operation on moderate hardware in harsh environments.

3. **Cross-Sell Opportunity** — Existing Talos Group clients in maritime, government, and healthcare represent an immediate addressable market for TalosVision. The trust and certification frameworks are already in place.

4. **Differentiated Positioning** — Unlike consumer-grade cloud surveillance platforms, TalosVision offers an **enterprise-grade, on-premises, certification-ready** alternative that aligns perfectly with Talos Group's brand promise.

5. **Recurring Revenue Model** — Per-installation licensing with multi-site aggregation (Spectral) creates a scalable, predictable revenue structure with strong expansion dynamics.
